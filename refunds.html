Refund and cancellation policy

How cancellations and refunds work for the course and our services.

Last updated 17 October 2026

The SharedLife Course

  • If the course is not right for you, ask for a full refund within 7 days of enrolling, as long as you have completed no more than 2 lessons.
  • After that, the course is non-refundable, because all lessons open as soon as you enrol.
  • If you were charged twice or paid but your lessons did not open, write to us and we will fix it or refund the extra payment in full.

Counselling and consultations

  • You can cancel a booking from your account at any time before it is confirmed, at no cost.
  • For a paid, confirmed session, cancel at least 24 hours before for a full refund or a free reschedule.

Pre-Nuptial Agreements and workshops

Fees and refund terms for Pre-Nuptial Agreements and workshops are agreed in writing before work begins.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Payments made by UPI or bank transfer are refunded to the same account.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write to advocateshravansharma@gmail.com or use the contact page.
