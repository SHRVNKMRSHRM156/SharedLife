About us

Marriage is about sharing happiness

SharedLife brings together family law, economics and counselling to help couples design marriages that last.

Our philosophy

Find someone who will make you happy.

Bring your own happiness, and build a life where it is shared.

When one partner is expected to keep the other happy, disappointment follows. We help two people each own their happiness, agree openly on money, roles, family and conflict, and write down what they decide.

What we stand for

Our values

⚖

Fair to both

Every conversation and every agreement is balanced. We work for the couple, not for one side.

✦

Confidential

What you share with us stays with us. Your agreement is held securely.

Built to last

Marriages change, so our support continues: reviews at life events and mediation when needed.
