Questions

About the course

Which languages is it in?

English and Hindi. Switch between them with one tap on any lesson, so each partner can read in the language they prefer.

Do we both need to enrol?

No. One enrolment covers a couple. Sit together and learn from one account, or share the account between you.

How do we pay?

By UPI, card, net banking or bank transfer when you enrol. All lessons open as soon as your payment is confirmed.

Can we get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days of enrolling if you have completed no more than 2 lessons. See our refund policy.

How long does it take?

About 9 hours in all. Most couples take one or two lessons a week, and each lesson ends with a short exercise to do together.

Can I take it on my own?

Yes. Many lessons are about knowing yourself first, and the exercises work as personal reflection too.

Is the law module legal advice?

No. It explains how marriage law and Pre-Nuptial Agreements work in India in general terms. For advice on your situation, book a consultation and we will connect you with an independent advocate.

Can we talk to someone as we go?

Yes. You can book counselling or a consultation from your account at any point in the course.
