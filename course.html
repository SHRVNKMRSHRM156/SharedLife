The SharedLife Course

Learn the skills of a lasting marriage

A 15-week course on marriage and relationships, for individuals and couples. It ends with each participant designing their own shared-life agreement.

15weeks
~45hours
8modules

Taught by specialists in family law and economics, with experienced counsellors leading the sessions on communication and conflict. Also offered as a 3-credit elective at Bennett University.

Capstone

Your own shared-life agreement

In the final weeks, each participant designs a written plan for their own relationship, using everything the course covers.
