Couples counselling

Talk it through, before it becomes a problem

Pre-marital and marital counselling with experienced counsellors who share one approach: two people, each owning their happiness, building one life.

Before the wedding

Pre-marital counselling

Guided conversations that bring hidden expectations into the open while they are still easy to discuss.

  • Money, savings, debts and spending habits
  • Careers, relocation and ambitions
  • Roles at home and with both families
  • Children and family planning
  • How you will handle disagreements
After the wedding

Marital counselling

Support for married couples at any stage, whether things are going well or feel strained.

  • Communication and recurring arguments
  • Adjusting to a new home or joint family
  • Becoming parents
  • Career changes and financial pressure
  • Rebuilding trust and closeness

How sessions work

Simple to start, built around you

Start with a free consultation. We match you with a counsellor and agree a plan: single sessions, or a package for deeper work.

  • Book per session, or as a package of sessions
  • Every counsellor is qualified, experienced with couples, and trained in the SharedLife approach
  • Everything you share stays confidential
  • Two counselling sessions are included with every Pre-Nuptial Agreement
