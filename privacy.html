Policies

Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we look after it.

Last updated 17 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email, mobile number and city, and anything optional you add, such as your partner's name or wedding date. For counsellors and advocates, professional details such as qualifications and registration numbers.
  • Bookings and course activity: the services you request, preferred times, notes you choose to share, your course enrolment and which lessons you have completed.
  • Payments: the amount, date and payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by our payment partner, Razorpay, and never reach us.

How we use it

  • To run your account, confirm bookings, give you course access and contact you about the services you asked for.
  • To match you with a counsellor or advocate on our panel, who sees only what they need for your session.
  • We do not sell your information or use it for third-party advertising.

Where it is kept

Your information is stored in a secure database hosted in Singapore by our hosting providers (Vercel and Neon). Passwords are stored only in encrypted (hashed) form.

Your choices

  • You can update your optional details from your account page.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account by writing to us. We may keep payment records where the law requires.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write to advocateshravansharma@gmail.com or use the contact page.
